PlumbTape Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · rockiib@icloud.com

This policy describes the submitted PlumbTape app, its in-app Privacy Policy, its one-time in-app purchase, and this public page.

English

PlumbTape has no user account, advertising, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated server that stores survey data. We do not sell or share personal data.

Camera frames are used only while AR corner capture is active. PlumbTape uses them to derive corner positions and does not save or upload the frames. Room geometry, door and window widths, furniture, notes, move-fit decisions, project names, unit and language preferences, and revisions stay in the app's local container.

PlumbTape Pro is one non-consumable in-app purchase. Apple processes payment, receipts, and restoration through StoreKit under Apple's privacy policy. PlumbTape receives only product and verified entitlement information needed to unlock Pro and does not receive full payment-card details. Loading, buying, verifying, or restoring the product may require a network connection.

Capture, calibration, fit checks, local storage, and PDF generation run on the device. The app does not upload survey data to a developer server. Opening this public policy is initiated by the user and happens in Safari.

Local projects remain until the user resets or deletes them in the app or deletes the app. Removing the app removes its local container, subject to device backups controlled by the user. PDFs and images shared outside the app are controlled by the user and the chosen destination.

Because PlumbTape does not operate a developer account or server containing survey data, access, correction, and deletion are performed directly in the app. For requests under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, China's Personal Information Protection Law, or another applicable privacy law, contact rockiib@icloud.com. We will respond as required by applicable law. No sale/share opt-out is needed because we do not sell or share personal data.

The app has no account, public content, advertising, or analytics and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If data practices change, this page and the in-app policy will be updated before the change takes effect.
