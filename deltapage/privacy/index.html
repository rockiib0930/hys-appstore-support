DeltaPage Privacy Policy

Effective: October 28, 2026

Local document processing

Imported document text, comparison results, decisions, notes, export files, and language preferences are stored in the app's local container. DeltaPage does not upload document content.

In-app purchase

DeltaPage Pro is one non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple StoreKit. DeltaPage receives only the verified entitlement status and never receives payment details. Loading, purchasing, verifying, or restoring a product may require a connection to Apple.

No tracking or analytics

DeltaPage has no developer-operated account system, cloud sync, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or tracking domain.

Deleting your data

Deleting a review removes it from the app. Removing DeltaPage removes its local container subject to iOS backup and system behavior. The verified Pro entitlement remains attached to the Apple account. There is no developer-side user profile to export or delete.

Contact

Privacy and support questions: codetea@163.com.

If data practices change, this public policy and the in-app Privacy Policy screen will be updated together before the changed behavior is released.
