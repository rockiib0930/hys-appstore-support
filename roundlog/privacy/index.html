RoundLog Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

RoundLog is designed for local inspection records. We do not create an account, sell data, show advertising or use analytics.

Inspection records

Stations, completed checks, notes, attachments and app preferences are stored in the app's local container. The developer does not receive them. Removing the app may remove this local data.

Camera

The camera is used only while the station QR scanner is visible. Frames are processed on the device to read a QR code and are not uploaded or retained.

Photo evidence

The system photo picker lets a user attach an image to an exception. Only the selected item is kept in local app storage.

Notifications

The weekly reminder is optional. If enabled, iOS schedules a local notification on the device. RoundLog does not use remote push notifications.

Purchases

Apple processes StoreKit purchases and restores. RoundLog receives product and entitlement status, not payment details. Purchase and restore can require a network connection.

Retention and control

The user controls the local records and can remove stations in the app. Because the developer does not hold a server copy, there is no developer-side deletion request to submit.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support link published on the App Store product page.
