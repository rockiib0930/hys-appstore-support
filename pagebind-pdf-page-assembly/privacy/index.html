PageBind Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

PageBind is a local PDF page-assembly tool. This policy explains what stays on the device and when Apple StoreKit is used.

Documents and local data

Imported PDFs, previews, page order, exclusions, export records and generated PDFs are stored in PageBind's Application Support container. PageBind does not upload documents to a developer server.

Purchase

PageBind Pro is one non-consumable purchase. Apple processes payment, restore and transaction verification. PageBind receives only the verified entitlement needed to unlock unlimited page exports.

Reminder

The daily reminder is off by default. If enabled, PageBind asks iOS for notification permission and schedules one local 18:30 reminder. Remote push is not used.

Accounts and tracking

There is no PageBind account, advertising, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking or sale of personal data.

Network access

Import, preview, reorder, rotation, exclusion, merge, split and export work offline. StoreKit may contact Apple for product, purchase or restore operations. The public policy page opens only after a tap.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until PageBind or its iOS container is deleted, subject to user-controlled backups. Files shared to another app are controlled by the user and the destination.
