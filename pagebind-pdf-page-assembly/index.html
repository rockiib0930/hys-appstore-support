Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

PageBind does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK or cross-app tracking.

Imported PDFs, page previews, page order, exclusions, export history and generated PDFs stay in the app's local Application Support container. PageBind does not upload document contents to a developer server.

PageBind Pro is one non-consumable in-app purchase. Apple processes payment, restoration and transaction verification through StoreKit. PageBind receives only the verified entitlement needed to unlock unlimited page exports.

PDF tools work offline. StoreKit may require a network connection for product loading, purchase or restore. The public policy page opens only after a user tap.

The daily reminder is optional and off by default. Enabling it asks iOS for notification permission and schedules one local reminder at 18:30. No remote push service is used.

Local data remains until the app or its iOS container is deleted, subject to user-controlled device backups. Files shared to another app are controlled by the user and that destination.
