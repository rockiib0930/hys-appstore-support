RetainRamp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

RetainRamp is a local-first spaced-repetition app. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data. There is no account, advertising, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated server.

Cards, answers, deck names, review history, language choice, reminder status, and purchase unlock state stay in the app's local container. Exported CSV and JSON files are created only after the user chooses an export action and are passed to the destination selected in the system file exporter.

RetainRamp Pro is one optional non-consumable in-app purchase. Apple processes payment and restoration through StoreKit under Apple's privacy policy. RetainRamp receives only the product and verified entitlement information needed to unlock the feature.

Daily reminders are off by default. When enabled, iOS requests notification permission and schedules a local notification on the device. No remote push service is used.

Local data remains until the user recovers or replaces a damaged library or removes the app. Removing the app removes its local container. There is no separate remote copy.
