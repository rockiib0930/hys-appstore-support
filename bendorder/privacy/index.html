BendOrder Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

BendOrder works offline. It does not create an account, track users across apps or sell personal information.

Project data

Run names, dimensions, completed bend orders, measured deviation and notes are stored in the app's local container. BendOrder does not upload this data.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro purchase is processed by Apple StoreKit. BendOrder receives a verified entitlement state, not payment details.

Network use

The workbench and calculations do not require a network connection. Apple may process App Store transactions under Apple's privacy policy.

Deletion

Delete a run inside the app or remove the app to delete its local data. Purchases can be restored from the Apple account used to buy them.

Children

BendOrder is a field layout aid for electrical work and is not directed to children.
