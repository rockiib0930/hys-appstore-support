KerfNest Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

KerfNest is a local-first sheet-goods planning tool. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves the device, and the choices available to you. It applies to the iOS app and this public policy page.

1. Data stored on the device

Project names, part dimensions, material stock, saved offcuts, cut preferences and generated layouts are stored in a local JSON file inside the app's Application Support directory. App language preference is stored with the system preferences API. This data is used only to provide the planning, layout, inventory and sharing features you request.

2. Data collection and use

KerfNest has no account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking domain, cloud sync or upload endpoint. The developer does not receive your parts list, material inventory or generated cut plans. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising or combine it with data from other apps or websites.

3. Sharing

Data leaves the app only when you explicitly choose Share and confirm a destination in the system share sheet.

4. Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app reads verified transaction entitlements and can restore purchases using App Store sync. Payment details are handled by Apple and are not available to the app. Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase, not a subscription.

5. Device permissions

KerfNest does not request access to the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, precise location or health data. The app does not require an account or login.

6. Retention and deletion

Local project data remains until you delete the app or remove the underlying system data. The app does not maintain a remote copy.

7. Your privacy rights

Because the developer does not receive your local project or inventory data, there is no server-side KerfNest profile to export or delete. You can remove local data by deleting the app. If you believe a GDPR, CCPA, China Personal Information Protection Law or similar request applies to information you sent to support, contact us and we will review the request.

8. Children

KerfNest is a workshop planning utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International transfers

KerfNest does not transmit project data to the developer. Apple independently handles App Store purchase processing under Apple's privacy terms; any transfer performed for that service is controlled by Apple.

10. Security

Local app data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and the device's normal data-protection controls. No method of storage is completely secure, so users should protect their device passcode and backups.

11. Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the matching in-app Privacy Policy screen will be updated before the changed behavior is released. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: support@dsh.local
