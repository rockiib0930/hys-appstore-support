Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

PanScale does not collect, sell, or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or tracking.

Recipes, ingredient lines, servings, pan dimensions, notes, custom densities, unit and language preferences, and unlock status stay in the app's local container. There is no developer-operated remote copy.

PanScale Pro is one non-consumable in-app purchase. Apple processes payment and restoration through StoreKit under Apple's privacy policy. PanScale receives only the verified entitlement status.

Recipe calculations work offline. The app does not upload recipes or settings to a developer server. StoreKit may require a network connection.

Local data remains until an item or the app is deleted. Deleting PanScale removes its local container; no separate remote copy exists. PanScale has no public user-generated content and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
